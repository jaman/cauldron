### Added

  shown frame by frame in a `Kino.Frame`. Takes `Cauldron2D.Parallax.Preview.frames/3`'s
  options with `:zoom` (default `2`) and `:fps` (default `20`).
* The `easel.livemd` notebook has a parallax section.
