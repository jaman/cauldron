# Changelog

## [0.4.0]

### Added

- Licensed under AGPL-3.0-only, with a commercial license for a game or service that does
  not publish its source: see `COMMERCIAL.md`.

- `Cauldron.SFX.Editor`: a bank's events in a terminal, auditioned and changed as you go.
- `mix cauldron.sfx` opens it on a bank file, or on the starter bank with no file.
- Depends on `cauldron_core` 0.4, where `Cauldron.SFX` — the bank, its variants and the
  function `Cauldron.Audio` plays — lives.
