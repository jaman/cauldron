# The Tower

Status: **idea, to be worked out after the overhaul in `GAMES_OVERHAUL.md`.**

A maze-running climb. Pac-Man and Bubble Bobble give the moment-to-moment play, the
Maze Runner novels the dread of a maze that does not want you out, and tower-climbing
manhua the shape of the whole: conquer a hundred floors and grow absurdly strong doing
it.

## The world

Towers fell from the sky. They are an advanced civilisation's way of terraforming a
planet. Their landing killed many, and the change they made to the world woke
something in some of the survivors. Those survivors are the players.

The floors of a tower are its terraforming machinery. Climbing them is how a player
gains power, and the higher floors show more of what the towers are for.

## Play

* **A floor is a maze** to clear, escape or survive, with the pace and readability of
  Pac-Man and Bubble Bobble: everything on screen at once, threats that move by rules
  a player can learn.
* **A hundred floors**, with bosses, and floors that change the rules: the walls move,
  the lights go out, the hunters become the hunted.
* **The power fantasy.** Early floors are deadly; later ones a player tears through,
  until the tower answers with something worse. Levels, skills and gear make the climb
  feel like the genre's escalation.
* **Classes** with abilities that change how a maze is played: one breaks walls, one
  sees the maze's layout, one moves through enemies, one commands them.
* **Companions** recruited on the way, with dialogue, loyalty and their own aims.
* **Other players** who help, compete, and betray.
